# Simplify the donation flow

## What will change
- Keep the existing choice between an NBCS donation and a Sankalpa offering.
- Remove the preset amount choices and all direct-payment app buttons.
- Show the donor form immediately, with one required amount field inside it.
- Place the QR code, UPI ID copy control, and QR save link after the donor details.
- Keep the UPI transaction ID as the final required field after payment, followed by “Submit for verification.”
- Preserve the existing committee verification page and stored submission details.

## Validation
- Require a whole-rupee amount between ₹1 and ₹10,00,000 in both the page and secure submission handler.
- Check the completed flow on a phone-sized screen and confirm the current build remains healthy.
